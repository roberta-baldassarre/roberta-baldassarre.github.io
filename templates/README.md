# Travel guide format

Use the Santa Rosa guide as the standard layout for all La Trottola trips.

Copy travel-guide.html into la-trottola/your-destination.html. Replace every DESTINATION, SUBTITLE and INTRO token. Paths are relative to la-trottola/.

Keep this order:
1. Editorial title, personal introduction and trip tags.
2. Color-coded timetable: time down the left, one column per trip day. Include activity names and brief practical notes.
3. Food and snack plan, interactive packing checklist, and before-you-leave checklist.
4. Detailed day-by-day notes, with times matching the timetable and relevant personal tips.
5. Before-you-go information, notebook sidebar and print button.

Use assets/site.css, assets/guide-planner.css and assets/site.js. The timetable must scroll horizontally on narrow screens and print legibly. Checkboxes last while the page stays open; do not imply they are saved.

Preserve approved trip content. Match the number of days to the actual trip. Label suggested times and keep draft labels where personal recommendations or practical details are unconfirmed. Do not invent visits, venues, hours or bookings. Remove placeholder prose before editorial launch. Add destination cards in explore.html and index.html for new guides. Do not publish the template itself as a finished guide.
