CALIFORNIA / COAST & CURIOSITY

Monterey with a preschooler.

Two days of jellyfish, bakery detours and really good food—with a four-year-old and a festival afternoon that became a favourite memory.

🚗 Drive from San Francisco: approximately 2–2½ hours
Direct drive, without stops. Allow extra time for weekend traffic and the Eataly stop in this itinerary; the coastal route takes longer.

Two days togetherAquarium & seaside walksOur festival-weekend notebook

ROBY’S EDIT

Why I’d choose this weekend.

The aquarium is the anchor, but the little moments make the trip: a dill scone after changing our breakfast plans, seals along the waterfront, and enough time to enjoy dinner outside. Our visit also included the Monterey Jazz Festival; that part needs its own dates and tickets.

At a glance

  • Length: two days, one night.
  • Our child’s age: four.
  • Starting point: San Francisco, with an Eataly stop in San Jose.
  • Our stay: Portola Hotel.
  • Pace: aquarium, waterfront walks and food stops, with a hotel break.

What worked for us

  • A central hotel for exploring Monterey on foot.
  • Changing bakeries when the first queue was too long.
  • A pool break between the aquarium and dinner.

What I’d plan around

  • The festival makes Day 2 a long day; our late drive home won’t suit every family.
  • Plan your picnic outside the aquarium under its visitor policy.
  • Keep The Adobe on the next-time list—we haven’t described it as a restaurant we tried.

Day 1 — Eataly + Aquarium + a Really Good Dinner 🐠

9:00 am

Leave San Francisco

First stop: Eataly San Jose to load up on sandwiches and Italian snacks for the drive and our aquarium picnic.

Our favourites:

  • Mulino Bianco cookies 🍪
  • Taralli + grissini
  • Focaccia
  • Italian sandwiches

La Trottola note: And, very importantly on a road trip with a little one: a convenient bathroom stop!

Lunch + afternoon

Monterey Bay Aquarium

We headed straight to the Monterey Bay Aquarium when we arrived in Monterey.

Instead of stopping somewhere for lunch, we ate our Eataly sandwiches outside on the aquarium deck overlooking the sea. Such an easy lunch with an incredible view.

Then plenty of time to explore the aquarium before heading to the hotel.

Our aquarium favourites: The jellyfish, puffins and tuna!

Planning your own picnic: That deck lunch is a memory from our visit. The aquarium’s current visitor policy prohibits outside food and drink, with limited exceptions. Plan your Eataly picnic outside the aquarium at a nearby spot such as San Carlos Beach or Berwick Park, and check the policy before you go.

4:00 pm

Check in at Portola Hotel

We stayed at Portola Hotel — a super convenient location for exploring Monterey on foot.

La Trottola note: The pool is heated — perfect for relaxing after the aquarium. There’s a hot tub to unwind in as well.

Dinner

Cella 🍷

Cella had incredible food and was perfect for eating outside. Monterey has so many good restaurants that it’s hard to choose!

Next time: We want to try The Adobe.

Day 2 — Bakery Crawl + Seals + Monterey Jazz Festival 🎷

Morning

Breakfast adventure

We walked over to Alta Bakery, but the line was too long, so we changed plans and headed to Ad Astra Bread Co.

And we loved it.

The breakfast sandwiches were packed with fresh herbs and so tasty. Don’t miss:

  • Savory dill scone 🌿
  • Double-baked almond croissant — incredibly crunchy
  • Lattes ☕️ — done just right

Late morning

Walk along the water 🦭

After breakfast, we strolled along the sea and stopped to see the seals hanging out below the deck.

Then a little souvenir shopping. My daughter’s favourites were the shells with the gold-painted edges and, of course, a lollipop. 🍭🐚

Afternoon + evening

Monterey Jazz Festival 🎶

We spent the rest of the day at the Monterey Jazz Festival.

Our absolute favourite was Joey Alexander — we loved him so much we saw him twice!

One of the most magical moments for my daughter was Joey Alexander’s collaboration with Amy Tan. Her stories about birds paired with his live jazz felt like story time on steroids — such a fun and unexpected experience for a four-year-old.

La Trottola note: This was a highlight of our festival weekend. Check the festival’s current dates, tickets and lineup when planning your own trip. The Amy Tan and Joey Alexander collaboration belongs to our visit, rather than a standing daily programme.

Our favourite festival food

Mixed kettle corn. Hands down. 🍿

Half caramel, half salty. Get the big bag.

9:00 pm

Drive back to San Francisco

We left the festival around 9 pm and had an easy drive home with no traffic.

That was our experience — keep your departure flexible for your family and the roads that evening.

🌀 La Trottola tip: Monterey works beautifully as a two-day getaway because you can park the car and do so much on foot: aquarium, bakeries, the waterfront, seals, little shops and great restaurants. Plan transport to the festival separately.

And definitely pack a picnic lunch for your aquarium day — eating outside overlooking the ocean was one of our favourite little moments of the weekend. For your visit, choose a nearby picnic spot outside the aquarium in line with its current food policy. 🌊

Before you go

Book aquarium tickets, check bakery hours and reserve dinner. Confirm hotel checkout and luggage storage for your second day. If jazz is part of the plan, build the weekend around the festival’s dates and allow time to get to and from the venue.
