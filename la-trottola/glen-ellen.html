CALIFORNIA / LITTLE WALKS & LONG LUNCHES

Glen Ellen:
A Little Day Escape

French pastries, a picnic under the trees, and a whole day to follow your curiosity. Two stops. Plenty of room to play.

One day from San FranciscoPastries & park trailsAt your family’s pace

A little France. A lot of fresh air.

A simple Sonoma day with just two destinations: breakfast in the village, then the rest of the day at Jack London State Historic Park. Let the picnic run long and the little discoveries set the pace.

8:30–9am / On the road

Good morning, Glen Ellen.

Leave San Francisco around 8:30–9am. Allow roughly 1½ hours for the drive, with extra time for traffic. There’s no packed schedule waiting at the other end.

Around 10–10:30am / Breakfast or brunch

Start with something buttery.

Make Les Pascals Patisserie et Boulangerie your first stop. Pick out pastries and coffee, settle into breakfast or brunch, and let the day begin slowly.

Think ahead to lunch: pick up bread and whatever savoury picnic options are available, plus a treat to share later. Bring water, fruit and a few favourite snacks from home, with a cool bag for anything perishable.

The yellow building at 13758 Arnold Drive has parking behind it. Les Pascals is closed on Wednesdays; choose another day for this two-stop outing. Check the bakery’s current hours before leaving.

Late morning / Up the hill

A park full of stories.

Drive up London Ranch Road to Jack London State Historic Park, at 2400 London Ranch Road. Pick up a map at the entrance and choose your stops around everyone’s energy.

Start at the House of Happy Walls Museum for a little of Jack and Charmian London’s story. Then explore Beauty Ranch: old barns, wide views and the wonderfully named Pig Palace. Make a game of spotting unusual farm buildings along the way.

Midday / Picnic, please

Unpack the good things.

Pause at an available designated picnic table near Beauty Ranch. Unwrap lunch, share the bakery treats and leave time to do very little. Ask staff about an available table if a picnic area is reserved.

After lunch, stop by Jack London’s Cottage during its visiting hours. Keep the ranch visit short or linger over the details—there’s no need to see everything.

Afternoon / One more discovery

Follow the trail to Wolf House.

If little legs still have some adventure left, explore the Wolf House ruins. Use the park map to plan this as a separate walk from the museum area, allowing time for the return and any uphill stretches. Ask staff which route suits your family.

Skip the extra walk if everyone is ready for a quieter afternoon. The museum, ranch and picnic already make a lovely day.

4–4:30pm / Homeward bound

A sleepy ride back.

Leave the park around 4–4:30pm and drive home to San Francisco. Allow roughly 1½ hours or more depending on traffic; this is your departure window, so there’s time for a final snack before setting off.

Before you go

The park currently lists daily hours of 9am–5pm, the museum 10am–5pm, and the Cottage noon–4pm. Check official hours, entry fees and accessibility information for holiday changes and visit-day details.

Pack water, picnic supplies, layers, sun protection and comfortable walking shoes. Paths and slopes vary: check the map and ask park staff about stroller access or mobility needs before choosing a trail.

A flexible park rhythm

House of Happy Walls → Beauty Ranch → Pig Palace → picnic → Cottage → optional Wolf House walk.

This is a suggested order, rather than one continuous loop. Use the park map to connect the areas and shorten the day whenever you like.

Small traveller mission

Find the Pig Palace. Spot a barn. Imagine a story set in the woods. Save the best discovery for the drive home.
