PLANNING TOOLS · TRIP CALENDAR

Two years of possibilities.

Make room for little adventures. Plan trips, school breaks and reminders across two years. Every day is editable.

Notes save automatically in this browser on this device. Choose a starting year to see two consecutive years.

Click or tap the space below any date to type or edit a note. Use your browser’s print dialog to save a PDF.

Highlighted dates mark U.S. federal holidays, including observed weekdays. Holiday reference: OPM ↗

Add a trip or event

Email reminders must be enabled in Google Calendar or your calendar provider after adding the event. This page does not send email.

Add to Google Calendar ↗

These options add a copy of the event; changes do not sync automatically. Open the downloaded file to add it to Apple Calendar.

Choose a tradition to add its main holidays across all two years.

Jewish and Islamic observances begin at sunset the previous evening. Islamic dates use the Umm al-Qura calendar and may vary with local moon sightings. About Islamic holiday dates ↗

Import a school or external calendar

Paste a public iCalendar (.ics) subscription link from your school or calendar provider. Imported events are saved in this browser; import again to refresh them.

If the provider blocks direct imports, download its .ics file and choose it here.

Imported events appear as labels; your daily notes stay editable. Repeating events must be exported as individual occurrences.
