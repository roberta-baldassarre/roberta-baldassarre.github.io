GAMES ON THE GO

Airport Bingo

Ages 3–7 · None preparation · Printable

Spot a suitcase, a plane, a window, a sign, a backpack and a trolley. Mark each square on the printable. Play together from your seat; stay with your grown-up.

Where: Airport, Airplane

Bring: Printed activity sheet, Pencil

GAMES ON THE GO

Restaurant I Spy

Ages 3–6 · None preparation · No supplies

Choose something everyone can see. Say “I spy something…” and give its colour or shape. Take turns guessing, then let the next person choose. Keep the game at your table.

Where: Restaurant, Hotel

Bring: Just your imagination

PRINT & PLAY

My Little Travel Journal

Ages 4–9 · 5 minutes preparation · Printable

Draw where you went, something you tasted and your favourite discovery. Write a sentence or ask a grown-up to take dictation. Add one page each day.

Where: Airplane, Car, Hotel, Rainy day

Bring: Printed activity sheet, Crayons, Pencil

OUTDOOR ADVENTURES

Nature Color Hunt

Ages 3–8 · None preparation · No supplies

Choose three colours and find something in each colour. Look, point or photograph your finds rather than picking them. Swap colours and try again. For younger children, find one colour together.

Where: Outdoors, Beach

Bring: Just your imagination

LITTLE MAKERS

DIY Paper Puppets

Ages 4–8 · 5 minutes preparation · Basic craft kit

Fold a strip of scrap paper into a standing zigzag character. Draw a face and decorate it. Make two characters, choose names and invent a tiny adventure. A grown-up handles any cutting.

Where: Hotel, Rainy day

Bring: Scrap paper, Crayons, Tape, Child scissors

OUTDOOR ADVENTURES

Beach Treasure Collection

Ages 3–8 · None preparation · No supplies

Make a collection of discoveries by spotting a smooth stone, a pattern, seaweed and three colours. Point, draw or photograph them. Leave shells, wildlife and natural objects where they belong; stay away from the water’s edge with your grown-up.

Where: Beach, Outdoors

Bring: Just your imagination

PRINT & PLAY

Postcards From My Trip

Ages 4–10 · 5 minutes preparation · Printable

Draw one little moment from today on the postcard front. On the back, write or dictate a message to someone you miss. Save it in your notebook or send it after your trip.

Where: Hotel, Restaurant, Rainy day, Car

Bring: Printed activity sheet, Crayons, Pencil

LITTLE MAKERS

Rainy Day Mini Theater

Ages 4–9 · 15 minutes preparation · Basic craft kit

Turn a small empty box on its side for a stage, or fold paper into a backdrop. Draw two characters and a setting. Tell a three-scene story: a surprise, a problem and a happy ending. Let everyone take a turn.

Where: Hotel, Rainy day

Bring: Recycled box or paper, Crayons, Tape

QUIET TIME · PAPER & CRAYONS

LITTLE MAKERS / ON THE GO

Leaves, rocks & sticks.

A few things at your feet, a little imagination, and five minutes together. Perfect for a picnic pause or a campsite afternoon.

❧

Leaf rubbings

You need: A fallen leaf, paper and a crayon.

Slip the leaf under the paper, vein-side up. Rub gently with the side of the crayon and watch its pattern appear. Try a second leaf in another colour.

5 MINUTES · PICNIC TABLE

◉

Rock creatures

You need: A few loose rocks, fallen leaves and little sticks.

Choose a rock for the body. Add leaf ears, stick legs and tiny pebble eyes around it. Is it a sleepy owl, a silly monster or a brand-new animal? Give it a name.

5–10 MINUTES · NO GLUE

✳

Stick picture frames

You need: Four fallen sticks, leaves and pebbles.

Lay the sticks in a square on the ground. Make a little scene inside: a leaf forest, a pebble family or a tiny campsite. Take a photo of your outdoor masterpiece.

5–10 MINUTES · MAKE & SNAP

☼

Nature faces

You need: Fallen leaves, small sticks and a few rocks.

Make a face on a clear patch of ground. Try leaf hair, rock cheeks and a curved stick smile. Take turns changing its expression: happy, surprised, very sleepy!

5 MINUTES · TAKE TURNS

◇

A tiny nature mandala

You need: Loose leaves, pebbles and short sticks.

Start with one pebble in the middle. Build rings around it, repeating colours or shapes. Each person adds one piece until your little circle feels complete.

5–10 MINUTES · QUIET PLAY

〰

Leaf boats

You need: A fallen leaf and a shallow bowl of water.

Float your leaf in the bowl and gently blow it across. Try different shapes: which one spins, which one sails? A tiny regatta right at the picnic table.

5 MINUTES · WATER PLAY

La Trottola tip: Keep paper and a few crayons in your day bag. Use only loose, fallen materials where collecting is allowed; leave living plants and wildlife undisturbed. Photograph your creations, put natural materials back where you found them, and take paper and supplies with you. Keep small pieces away from little ones who still put things in their mouths.
