Day 1 — Play + Pool 💦

10:00 am

Children’s Museum

Start at the Children’s Museum of Sonoma County. Water play, climbing, pretend play and outdoor activities — such a fun morning for little ones.

Pro tip: Before buying museum tickets, check Discover & Go through the San Francisco Public Library for free admission passes to the Children’s Museum of Sonoma County. Sign in with your library card to see available dates and the admission details for your pass — a little library-card magic for your weekend!

12:30 pm

Lunch in Railroad Square

Lunch at Grossman’s Noshery, then take a little walk around Railroad Square.

2:30 pm

Check-in + Pool

Check in at the Sandman Hotel.

We originally wanted to stay at the Flamingo, but it was fully booked — and that’s how we discovered the Sandman! It’s one of the more affordable options in Santa Rosa, with a fun pool area and a hot tub that’s perfect for relaxing.

Good to know: the pool isn’t the warmest, but the hot tub makes up for it!

🍎 Snack stop: Trader Joe’s is only two blocks from the hotel, making it super easy to stock up on snacks, fruit and drinks.

Spend the rest of the afternoon by the pool.

5:30 pm

Dinner

Dinner at The Spinster Sisters. Great food in a relaxed setting. Make a reservation.

Day 2 — A Very Snoopy Day 🐶

Morning

Hockey 🏒

Start the day at Snoopy’s Home Ice and catch a hockey game.

Late morning

Schulz Museum

Walk next door to the Charles M. Schulz Museum for all things Snoopy, Charlie Brown and Peanuts.

Lunch

Snoopy’s Home Ice 🍕

Head back to the rink for an easy lunch before skating.

Afternoon

Ice Skating ⛸️

Now it’s your turn on the ice!

They have flipped buckets to hold onto while learning to skate — great for little ones and adults who are just getting started.

Late afternoon

Pool + Hot Tub 💦

Back to the Sandman for another swim and some hot-tub time.

Dinner

Bird & The Bottle 🍽️

Dinner at Bird & The Bottle. Go early with little ones — reservation recommended.

Day 3 — Easy Morning + Home ☕️

Breakfast

One last easy morning

Breakfast at the hotel. It’s not great, but it’s convenient before getting on the road.

Then pack up and drive back to San Francisco. 🚗

A little tip for foodie parents: Healdsburg is just a quick drive from Santa Rosa, with Michelin-starred dining worth planning a special evening around — including SingleThread. Book well ahead for a little grown-up indulgence.

Before you go

Check current opening hours, reservations, hockey games and public skating sessions before you set out. Confirm skating aids with the rink, and leave a little room in the day for a rest.
