CIAO, I’M ROBY

A little curiosity.
A little less planning.

La Trottola is my notebook of little adventures from San Francisco, with young children in tow.

It started with notes for friends: baby essentials, birthday ideas, places to go and what to pack. I like having the useful details in one place—and choosing what makes life easier over what looks perfect.

I’m starting with a small collection of California getaways, with useful details and room for a change of plan. Alongside them are San Francisco resources, simple games and checklists to make the organising easier.

I’d rather grow this notebook thoughtfully than fill it quickly. Personal notes and researched options both have a place here; a listing alone does not mean I’ve visited.

Take what works for your family, leave room for a detour, and always make a little time to play.
