A little curiosity.
A little less planning.

La Trottola is my growing notebook of family adventures, practical lists and little discoveries worth sharing.

It started with notes for friends: baby essentials, birthday ideas, places to go and what to pack. I like having the useful details in one place—and choosing what makes life easier over what looks perfect.

Here you’ll find San Francisco resources, California getaways, simple games and checklists you can take anywhere. Some come from personal experience; others are researched starting points for your own plans.

Take what works for your family, leave room for a detour, and always make a little time to play.
