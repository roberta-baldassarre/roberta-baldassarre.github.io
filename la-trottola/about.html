La Trottola is a family travel journal taking shape around a simple idea: a good trip leaves room for curiosity, play, and a change of plan.

A notebook, growing slowly.

We’re beginning with California: Calistoga, Monterey, Santa Rosa, and Glen Ellen. Alongside the guides, you’ll find small games for the journey and practical packing lists.

A note from the editor.

This is an early edition. Destination guides are clearly marked as drafts while personal recommendations and original photographs are gathered. Roberta’s story and more family travel notes will follow.
