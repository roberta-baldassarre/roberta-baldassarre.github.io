Family life.
Little adventures.

A practical family guide rooted in San Francisco, with California getaways and ideas you can take anywhere.

A little less organising.

Planning a birthday, choosing a camp or packing for two nights away can take more time than the adventure itself. La Trottola brings places, ideas and useful lists together so you have somewhere to start.

Three ways to begin.

Places brings together San Francisco playground resources, camps, birthday venues and California itineraries. Play offers simple games and crafts. Planning tools keeps checklists and calendars close at hand.

From Roby’s notebook.

The collection includes personal notes, researched resources and links to providers. A listing is a starting point for your planning; it does not mean every place has been personally visited. Check current details directly before booking.

Start close to home, leave room for a detour, and take the ideas that work for your family.
