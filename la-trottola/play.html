GAMES · ANYWHERE

A little fun, wherever you are.

No elaborate supplies. No perfect results. Just something fun to do together.

✳

Colour hunt

Choose a colour. Take turns spotting five things in that colour along the way. Play from your seat or during a walking break.

ON THE GO · NO SUPPLIES

〰

One-line story

Start with “One day, a tiny traveller…” Each person adds one sentence. See where your story wanders.

ANYWHERE · 2 OR MORE PLAYERS

◇

Postcard from today

Fold a sheet of paper. Draw one thing you noticed, then dictate or write a message on the back. Keep it in your travel notebook.

QUIET TIME · PAPER & CRAYONS

LITTLE MAKERS / ON THE GO

Leaves, rocks & sticks.

A few things at your feet, a little imagination, and five minutes together. Perfect for a picnic pause or a campsite afternoon.

❧

Leaf rubbings

You need: A fallen leaf, paper and a crayon.

Slip the leaf under the paper, vein-side up. Rub gently with the side of the crayon and watch its pattern appear. Try a second leaf in another colour.

5 MINUTES · PICNIC TABLE

◉

Rock creatures

You need: A few loose rocks, fallen leaves and little sticks.

Choose a rock for the body. Add leaf ears, stick legs and tiny pebble eyes around it. Is it a sleepy owl, a silly monster or a brand-new animal? Give it a name.

5–10 MINUTES · NO GLUE

✳

Stick picture frames

You need: Four fallen sticks, leaves and pebbles.

Lay the sticks in a square on the ground. Make a little scene inside: a leaf forest, a pebble family or a tiny campsite. Take a photo of your outdoor masterpiece.

5–10 MINUTES · MAKE & SNAP

☼

Nature faces

You need: Fallen leaves, small sticks and a few rocks.

Make a face on a clear patch of ground. Try leaf hair, rock cheeks and a curved stick smile. Take turns changing its expression: happy, surprised, very sleepy!

5 MINUTES · TAKE TURNS

◇

A tiny nature mandala

You need: Loose leaves, pebbles and short sticks.

Start with one pebble in the middle. Build rings around it, repeating colours or shapes. Each person adds one piece until your little circle feels complete.

5–10 MINUTES · QUIET PLAY

〰

Leaf boats

You need: A fallen leaf and a shallow bowl of water.

Float your leaf in the bowl and gently blow it across. Try different shapes: which one spins, which one sails? A tiny regatta right at the picnic table.

5 MINUTES · WATER PLAY

La Trottola tip: Keep paper and a few crayons in your day bag. Use only loose, fallen materials where collecting is allowed; leave living plants and wildlife undisturbed. Photograph your creations, put natural materials back where you found them, and take paper and supplies with you. Keep small pieces away from little ones who still put things in their mouths.

OUTDOOR PLAY

A playground pause.

Sometimes the simplest outing is the best part of the day.

Pick a playground that suits your children’s ages and energy. Before heading out, check the park’s current opening information, toilets, shade and accessible routes. Bring water, a snack and an extra layer, and leave a little time for one more slide.

SCHOOL-BREAK ADVENTURES

Find their kind of camp.

Start with what sparks their curiosity: making, moving, exploring or being outdoors.

When comparing camps, check age groups, session dates, daily hours, location, cost and cancellation terms. Ask about the daily rhythm, staff supervision, accessibility, meals and how the team helps children settle in.

A short list of your family’s priorities makes it easier to find a good fit.

LITTLE CELEBRATIONS

Make it their kind of day.

Start with something they love, add a few friends, and keep a little room for play.

A park picnic, a craft table or an afternoon of favourite games can be all you need. Choose a place and a pace that suit your child, then build the celebration around one or two easy activities.

  • Choose your date, guest list and budget.
  • Check booking requirements, toilets and an indoor or rainy-day backup.
  • Ask guests about food allergies and access needs when you send invitations.
  • Keep food simple, bring water, and plan a moment for cake.
  • Pack supplies for the activities, rubbish bags and something to take leftovers home.

For an easy activity, try a colour hunt or a story game, or set up a little nature craft together.
