Before you leave

Read the day-by-day notes ↓

Saturday — Sonoma → Calistoga 🚂

9:00 am

Leave San Francisco

Drive about one hour to Sonoma. It’s an easy way to break up the journey, with a little adventure before heading farther north. Leave extra time for weekend traffic.

10:00 am

Sonoma TrainTown

Start the weekend with the miniature train ride at Sonoma TrainTown — a fun first stop with young kids.

12:00 pm

Lunch in Sonoma Square

We love Butcherman BBQ and Sonoma Cheese Factory. Informal, easy to order for young ones and grown-ups, and easy to eat on the covered patio.

1:00 pm

Park time + ice cream

Relax in Sonoma Plaza, the park across the street. No need to hurry back into the car. Around 1:45 pm, stop at Sweet Scoops for ice cream before leaving Sonoma.

2:15 pm

Drive to Calistoga

Back in the car for about another hour to Calistoga, depending on traffic.

3:15 pm · Adjust to hotel check-in

Check in + pool 💦

We stayed at Calistoga Motor Lodge. Our favourite way to settle in: straight to the heated, kid-friendly pool. There was also a separate adults-only pool.

La Trottola note: Pool time is part of the itinerary. Leave the afternoon open and confirm current pool access and amenities with the hotel.

6:00 pm

An easy hotel dinner

Dinner at the hotel is convenient after the drive and a swim, although it wasn’t our favourite meal of the trip. Sometimes easy is exactly what everyone needs.

7:30 pm

S’mores + a glass of wine 🔥

Buy a s’mores kit at the hotel or bring your own. Sit around the fire pit, toast marshmallows and sip a glass of wine while the kids enjoy their treat.

La Trottola note: One of those very simple vacation moments we love.

8:15 pm

One little bocce game

Play a game of bocce before heading to bed. Follow the kids’ energy — this can be a very short game!

Sunday — Petrified Forest + Calistoga 🌲

8:30 am

Breakfast at Sarmentine

Walk from the hotel to Sarmentine for pastries and breakfast. An easy start before your little hike.

10:00 am

Petrified Forest

Visit the Petrified Forest for a small, manageable hike and something a little different for the kids to explore.

12:30 pm

Lunch at Calistoga Inn

Head back into town for a relaxed lunch at the Calistoga Inn restaurant.

2:00 pm

Pioneer Park 🛝

Chill in Pioneer Park and let the kids enjoy the playground. A little downtime before heading back to the hotel.

3:30 pm

Pool afternoon

Back to the hotel to relax by the pool. Keep this stretch of the day open for swimming, a rest and whatever everyone needs.

6:00 pm · Suggested dinner time

Dinner at Lovina

Dinner at Lovina to finish the day.

La Trottola tip: Make your reservation at least one week ahead — especially for a long weekend. Book Lovina ↗

Monday — Geyser + Napa Valley Lunch 🐐

8:00 am

Calistoga Roastery

Coffee at Calistoga Roastery and an easy start to the morning.

9:00 am

Old Faithful Geyser

A fun, quick outing before the day gets going at Old Faithful Geyser of California.

La Trottola tip: Buy some animal food at the entrance! The kids can feed the goats and llamas while you’re there — a surprisingly fun part of the visit.

10:30 am

One last swim

Back to the hotel for a final dip in the pool while one parent packs the car.

12:00 pm

Check out

Confirm your hotel’s checkout time and pool access when planning that final swim.

12:30 pm

Lunch — choose your route

TRUSS
Our pick for the beautiful mountain views. And we loved the burger.

or

Farmstead at Long Meadow Ranch · St. Helena
A great alternative farther south on the way back to San Francisco.

Optional · Reserve ahead

One more adventure? 🏰

If everyone still has energy, add a winery stop before heading home. We wanted to check out Castello di Amorosa, mostly for the incredible castle architecture! Our daughter is very into castles these days, so this felt like a fun way to make a Napa winery stop interesting for her too.

Good to know: This was on our wish list. Castello requires reservations for everyone to enter, including children. Choose a family-appropriate visit; shared guided tours are for ages 21 and over, and strollers aren’t allowed inside.

Then, when everyone is officially done adventuring: back to San Francisco. 🚗

Before you go

Check current attraction hours and restaurant reservations for your dates. Reserve Lovina early, confirm hotel amenities and pool access, and book Castello ahead if it’s part of your plan. Keep the timing flexible for little legs, traffic and one more swim.
