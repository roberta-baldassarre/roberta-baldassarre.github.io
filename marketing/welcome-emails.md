## Email 2 — three days later

Subject: Coast, pools or pastries?

A getaway doesn’t need an ambitious itinerary. Start with the part everyone will enjoy:

- [Monterey](https://roberta-baldassarre.github.io/la-trottola/monterey.html): aquarium discoveries and seaside food stops over two days.
- [Calistoga](https://roberta-baldassarre.github.io/la-trottola/calistoga.html): three days with trains, pool time and little discoveries.
- [Santa Rosa](https://roberta-baldassarre.github.io/la-trottola/santa-rosa.html): a Snoopy-filled weekend with room for the pool.
- [Glen Ellen](https://roberta-baldassarre.github.io/la-trottola/glen-ellen.html): pastries and a walk in Jack London State Historic Park, as a day trip.

Borrow what works and leave room to change your mind. Check official venue websites for current booking details before you go.

Roby
